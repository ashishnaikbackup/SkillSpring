HOW TO LEARN HERE

One lesson at a time

  1. Read the concept and vocabulary.
  2. Study the worked example and predict its output.
  3. Re-type and modify the example yourself.
  4. Complete the practice task without copying.
  5. Check your understanding and mark the lesson complete.
  6. Build the module project and keep your work in GitHub.
