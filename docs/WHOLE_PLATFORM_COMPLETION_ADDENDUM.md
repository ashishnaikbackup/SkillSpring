# Whole-platform learning expansion addendum — 1 November 2026

## What changed

- The published course catalogue now has **30 entries across the 12 learning schools**.
- Five additional six-lesson beginner courses cover:
  - Business and accounting
  - IT support, networking and cloud foundations
  - Mathematics, science and research foundations
  - Creative media, design and communication
  - Personal productivity and knowledge management
- The [Career Launchpad](../career-launchpad.html) contains one guided capstone for every catalogue course. Each project brief includes a role direction, staged tasks, portfolio evidence and an interview prompt.
- The [Professional Practice Labs](../professional-labs.html) now include seven tracks: spreadsheet modelling, descriptive data analysis, electronics, networking, UI/UX review, personal cash-flow budgeting and business unit economics/break-even.
- The [Progress Backup](../progress-backup.html) page provides local JSON export/import so learners can move progress manually between browsers without creating accounts or sending learning data to a server.
- The [AI-assisted source audit](COURSE_CONTENT_AUDIT_2026-11-01.md) inventories the actual 30 courses, lesson counts, strengths and remaining depth gaps.

## Recommended release wording

SkillSpring is a beginner-first learning and portfolio-preparation platform with 30 course entries, 12 learning schools, practical labs, course-specific capstone briefs and local progress portability. It is not an accredited qualification, a substitute for supervised specialist training, a complete professional textbook in every field, or a guarantee of employment.

## Final acceptance tasks

1. A human reviewer with relevant subject expertise should verify each course's facts, worked examples, answer keys, safety/privacy/licensing notes and references. Record reviewer identity/role, date, findings and corrections. Do not set a review date before this happens.
2. Run every practical workflow in the required desktop/browser tools and test all answer keys and edge cases.
3. Conduct manual Android and desktop checks, keyboard-only navigation, zoom/reflow, reduced-motion and screen-reader testing with actual assistive technology.
4. Expand short foundation courses into deeper specialisation tracks based on real entry-level job descriptions and verified employer expectations.
5. Ask learners/mentors to review the projects and record feedback. A brief alone is not proof that a learner can perform the task.

These tasks are intentionally visible. Do not close the master completion issue or describe every course as professionally complete until the acceptance evidence exists.
