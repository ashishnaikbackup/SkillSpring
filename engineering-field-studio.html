ENGINEERING SCHOOL · FIELD PRACTICE

Engineering is more than one branch.
Learn it by solving problems.

Five beginner-friendly applied studios for mechanical, civil, chemical, instrumentation and biomedical engineering. Each starts with a plain-language concept, shows a worked example, lets you change the inputs and checks an independent task. The models are deliberately simplified so you can understand the mathematics before moving to professional software, standards and supervised laboratories.

5 field studios•Worked examples•Instant practice feedback
Important: These are educational calculations using idealised or fictional data. They are not design approvals, medical guidance, process operating instructions or substitutes for codes, datasheets, qualified supervision, validated software or laboratory training. Never use these results to build, construct, operate or treat anything in the real world.

From practice to portfolio

After each studio, turn the calculation into a small engineering evidence pack. A correct number alone is not a professional project.

Beginner · Explain one model

Write the problem, draw a labelled diagram, list assumptions, show the equation and units, calculate the result, test a second input and explain one limitation.

Intermediate · Compare scenarios

Build a spreadsheet or small program that compares at least three scenarios. Add input validation, a boundary case, a chart or table, and a short sensitivity discussion.

Advanced · Validate a workflow

Choose a field-specific tool, consult its official documentation, reproduce a benchmark example, compare results, record version and settings, perform verification checks and defend the limitations.

Shared assessment rubric · 100 points

  • Correct model and units — 20
  • Reasoning and stated assumptions — 20
  • Independent practice and boundary tests — 20
  • Validation, safety and limitations — 20
  • Clear diagrams, reproducible files and explanation — 20
